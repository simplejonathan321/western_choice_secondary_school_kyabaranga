About Western Choice Secondary School

Western Choice Secondary School is a mixed school catering for both day and boarding sections where the boarding section is complusary for candidate classes (s.4 & s.6) It is a premier educational institution committed to academic excellence and holistic development. Our state-of-the-art campus provides a nurturing environment where students can thrive both academically and personally.

With a team of highly qualified educators and modern facilities, we offer a comprehensive curriculum that prepares students for higher education and future leadership. Our programs emphasize critical thinking, creativity, and character development, ensuring well-rounded graduates ready to make a positive impact in society.

At Western Choice, we believe in fostering a community of lifelong learners who are equipped with the knowledge, skills, and values to succeed in an ever-changing world.
